Substitute:
  s/CONTENT TO BE REPLACED/CONTENT TO REPLACE/
  * s = substitute
    * Prefixes:
      * -: one line before the cursor (-s)
      * +: one line after the cursor (+s)
      * .: current line (the line where the cursor is on) (.s)
      * #: line at # number (1s, 5s, 13s, etc)
      * %: Operate in the entire file (%s)

  Suffixes (you may use more than one suffix like /gc):
    g = Global (s/content/replacement/g)
    c = Case insensitive (s/content/replacement/c)
    C = Case sensitive (s/content/replacement/C)

  Replace Visual-Selection
    \%VCONTENT (it's better to use a macro)

  Select Number:
    \d\+

  Select "Everything":
    .*

  Append (Like ".." from lua or "+" from most programming languages)
    '.' between two "vars"

  Create a capture group (submatch):
    \(CONTENT\)
      Examples:
        \(\d\+\)
        \(.*\)
        \(%VCONTENT\)

    Each capture group is ++ than the previous group, so \(\d\+\) \(.*\) \(%VCONTENT\):
      \(\d\+\) = (submatch(1))
      \(.*\) = (submatch(2))
      \(%VCONTENT\) = (submatch(3))


  Multiply Visual-Selection
    s/\(\d\+\)/\=submatch(0)*10/

  Multiply two numbers with large space between them and output to a third column
    .s/\(\d\+\)\(.*\)\(\d\+\).*/\=submatch(1) . submatch(2) . submatch(3) . " " . submatch(1)*submatch(3)*3/
      So if my line is like this:
        5   6
          5 is the first \(\d\+\) the space between 5 and 6 is \(.*\) and 6 is the second \(\d\+\)
          There are three groups, \(\d\+\), \(.*\) and \(\d\+\), so submatch(1), submatch(2), submatch(3)
          So 5 is submatch(1), the empty space is submatch(2) and 6 is  submatch(3)
          the "\=" after "/" is telling to evaluate the expression that follows it.
          so \=submatch(1) . submatch(2) . submatch(3) . " " . submatch(1)*submatch(3)*3 will look like this internally
              \=5 . "   " . 6 . " " . 5*6*3 wich will become "5   6 90"
